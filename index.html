    The executive

    Nine years leading A&R at Universal Music Canada, where he rose to Senior Vice-President and founded the B.L.A.C.K. Label Coalition. Since 2023, Global A&R at Def Jam, signing and developing artists anywhere in the world. His own label, Black Stone Colleagues, releases his records.

    He also speaks: on staying original, building culture through collaboration, and longevity in a creative career.
